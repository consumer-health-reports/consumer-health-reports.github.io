Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

At Consumer Health Reports (accessible at https://consumer-health-reports.github.io/), protecting visitor privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our portal and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration, nor do we directly collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, home addresses, or payment details on this website. Any purchase transactions occur exclusively on the official external websites of the respective product manufacturers.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate visitor behavior, such as page views, session duration, device types, and referral sources. Google Analytics uses cookies and anonymized identifiers to compile statistical reports that help us improve site performance and editorial relevance. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

3. Third-Party & Affiliate Links

Our product reviews contain outbound links to official manufacturer websites and authorized retailers. Clicking these links may place a referral cookie on your browser so the vendor can attribute the visit to Consumer Health Reports. Once you leave our domain, we have no control over the privacy practices, cookies, or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy of any third-party site you visit.

4. Data Security & Hosting

This website is statically hosted via GitHub Pages over encrypted HTTPS connections. While no internet transmission is 100% immune to risk, static architecture minimizes attack surfaces by eliminating server-side user databases.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data collected via tracking technologies, as well as the right to opt out of non-essential cookies through your browser preferences.

Joint Mend Ingredients — Pharmacology & Formulation Audit

Below is our technical breakdown of the seven active compounds declared in the official Joint Mend formulation and their physiological roles in joint nutrition:

Cartilage Structure

1. Glucosamine Sulfate

An endogenous amino monosaccharide that serves as a primary biochemical precursor for glycosaminoglycans and proteoglycans in articular cartilage, supporting joint shock absorption and structural integrity.

Inflammatory Balance

2. Boswellia Extract (Boswellia serrata)

Standardized gum resin extract rich in boswellic acids (such as AKBA), researched for modulating 5-lipoxygenase (5-LOX) pathways to support joint comfort and everyday mobility.

Cartilage Hydration

3. Chondroitin Sulfate

A sulfated glycosaminoglycan naturally present in connective tissue that helps retain water within the cartilage matrix, supporting elasticity and resistance to compression.

Connective Tissue & Sulfur

4. MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)

A bioavailable organosulfur compound that provides elemental sulfur for collagen cross-linking and supports the body's natural response to physical stress in joints and tendons.

Enzymatic Recovery

5. Bromelain

A proteolytic enzyme complex derived from pineapple stems (Ananas comosus), widely utilized in joint and sports nutrition to support soft-tissue recovery after physical exertion.

Antioxidant Defense

6. Quercetin

A plant flavonol with free-radical scavenging properties that pairs with Bromelain to support oxidative balance and cellular resilience in heavily used joints.

Amino Acid Precursor

7. Methionine (L-Methionine)

An essential sulfur-containing amino acid required for protein synthesis and endogenous production of S-adenosylmethionine (SAMe), supporting healthy cartilage and connective tissue maintenance.

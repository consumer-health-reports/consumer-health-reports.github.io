About Us & Editorial Standards

Our mission: Providing transparent, evidence-based evaluations of modern dietary formulations and consumer wellness protocols.

Who We Are & Our Objective

Consumer Health Reports was created to cut through digital marketing noise and aggressive supplement claims. In an industry flooded with hidden "proprietary blends," inflated dosage figures, and unverified reviews, our goal is simple: deliver clear, objective, and clinically supported information to help consumers make educated wellness choices.

We specialize in dissecting over-the-counter wellness products, sublingual drops, and specialized joint, nerve, and metabolic support protocols. We examine exactly what is inside the bottle, cross-reference dosages with published literature, and report on real user experiences.

Our 4-Point Formulation Verification Protocol

Every product reviewed on this portal undergoes an evaluation covering four primary benchmarks:

1. Label Transparency

We favor formulations that disclose exact milligram amounts for every active botanical or mineral over opaque proprietary blends that conceal individual doses.

2. Clinical Plausibility

We cross-reference active ingredients with clinical databases (including PubMed and clinical pharmacology resources) to ensure ingredients match evidence-backed biological actions.

3. Facility Standards (GMP & FDA)

We prioritize products manufactured in USA-based, FDA-registered facilities operating under current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) for purity and contamination prevention.

4. Authenticity & Buyer Protection

We investigate refund reliability, verify official distribution channels, and warn buyers against unsealed or unauthorized third-party marketplace clones.

Affiliate Disclosure & Editorial Independence

In accordance with FTC guidelines, we believe in full financial transparency. Consumer Health Reports is reader-supported. When you purchase a supplement through external links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra charge to you.

However, commercial partnerships never dictate our editorial verdicts:

  • We consistently outline product drawbacks, timelines (most formulas require 60–90 days), and demographic restrictions.
  • We never accept direct payment to alter scorecard ratings or suppress negative feedback.
  • Every outbound commercial link strictly adheres to search engine standards using rel="nofollow sponsored noopener".

Medical Disclaimer

The content on Consumer Health Reports is for informational and educational purposes only and is not intended as medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Dietary supplements are not evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider before introducing any new supplement protocol into your health regimen, particularly if you are managing pre-existing health conditions or taking prescription medications.
